# Gather logo

Created with the built-in Imagegen tool. The transparent forest-green emblem
combines a cross with people gathering. The existing Gather wordmark remains
live HTML text so it stays accessible and sharp.

- `gather-logo.png`: original 1254 × 1254 transparent PNG.
- `gather-icon-192.png`: resized icon used by the sidebar, sign-in brand and touch icon.
- `gather-icon-32.png`: resized browser favicon.

The two smaller icons were resampled from the original without changing the
design or removing alpha. Local/Vercel allowlists publish only these three PNGs;
this design note is excluded from deployment.

## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one original standalone logo emblem for Gather, a worship and ministry planning web app, used beside an existing live-text wordmark and as a browser favicon.
Primary request: create a polished, minimalist, memorable icon expressing Christian worship and a welcoming community gathering. A simple upright cross forms the quiet center; broad curved leaf or embracing human forms gather around it into an open circular silhouette. Make it feel thoughtfully designed, balanced, calm, and warm.
Style: crisp flat vector-like shapes, clean geometric curves, solid forest green close to #335a46 only. Rounded forms and confident proportions. Strong negative space, readable at small sizes.
Composition: a single centered icon on a square transparent canvas, generously fills about 80 percent of the frame with even transparent margins, no clipping. No wordmark or text; the app supplies the Gather typography separately.
Constraints: genuine transparent background with preserved alpha; no gradients, shadows, glow, textures, mockup, border, badge background, tiny details, watermarks, or extra symbols. Deliver just the emblem.
```
