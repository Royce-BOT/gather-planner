# Gather — User Guide

Gather helps your church organize worship services, programs, volunteers, and songs. Use this guide to browse schedules, prepare a service, and manage your church workspace.

## Browse worship schedules

1. Open your church's Gather website. You can browse published content without an account.
2. Use **Overview** to find the next service, or open **Service planner** to browse schedules.
3. Choose **Current plans** for upcoming and recently finished services, or **Past programs** for older services. Use the search box to find an entry in the selected list.
4. Choose **Open program** on a service to see its theme, program order, durations, and any publicly shared assignments.
5. Choose **Print program** to open your browser's print options. You can print on paper or save a PDF if your browser supports it.
6. Open **Song library** to browse published song titles.

Choose **Refresh schedules** to check for updated public content. If no schedules are listed, your church may not have published any yet. Use **Try again** if schedules fail to load.

## Register or sign in

### Use an email account

1. Choose **Register** in the header.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and a password of 8–128 characters. Enter the same password in **Confirm password**.
3. Choose **Create account**.
4. Open the verification email and follow its link. Check your spam folder if necessary.
5. Return to Gather and choose **Sign in** with your email and password.

### Use a Google account

1. Choose **Sign in** or **Register**.
2. Choose **Continue with Google**.
3. Select your Google account and complete Google's sign-in steps to return to Gather.

After your first verified sign-in, a church admin must approve your access. While **Awaiting admin approval** appears, you can continue browsing public schedules. Choose **Check status** after an admin reviews your request. If your request is declined or your access is revoked, contact your church admin; public browsing remains available.

Choose **Back to public schedules** to close the account screen. Choose **Log out** in the header when you finish using your account; you can continue browsing public content.

### Reset a forgotten password

1. Choose **Sign in**, then **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and choose **Send reset link**.
3. Follow the link in your email and enter a new password twice.
4. Choose **Save new password**, then sign in with your new password.

If a reset link has expired, request a new one.

## Check what your account can do

| Your access | Available actions |
| --- | --- |
| Visitor or account awaiting approval, declined, or revoked | Browse and print published services and browse published song titles. |
| Approved viewer | Read and print the full church planner, view volunteers, and export a private backup. |
| Approved editor | Viewer actions, plus add, edit, and delete planner entries, publish content, and import backups. |
| Approved admin | Editor actions, plus approve requests and manage member roles and access. |

Signing in alone does not enable editing. Ask your church admin if you need a different role.

## Plan or edit a service

These actions require an approved **Editor** or **Admin** account.

1. Open **Service planner** and choose **Plan a service**.
2. Enter the **Service name**, **Date**, and **Start time**.
3. Add the theme, church member location, and preparation notes as needed.
4. Leave publication unchecked while preparing a private plan. Follow the publishing instructions below when it is ready for visitors.
5. Choose **Save changes** and wait for **Saved**.
6. Choose **Open program** to prepare the service order.

To change an existing service, choose **Edit** on its card or **Edit service** inside its program. To delete a service, choose **Delete** on its card and confirm the prompt. Use **Past programs** to find an older service before editing it.

Each successful save shows **Saved**, which fades away after a few seconds. Wait for the result before continuing. Choose **Refresh data** before editing if another person may have changed the planner.

## Build the service program

1. Open a service and choose **Add program item**.
2. Enter the item title and a duration of 1–300 whole minutes. If needed, enter an assigned volunteer or team; saved volunteer names appear as suggestions.
3. Leave the public-assignment option unchecked unless that name or assignment should be visible to visitors.
4. Choose **Save changes**. Repeat for each part of the service.
5. Use **Edit** beside an item to change it. Use **↑** to move an item up one position; repeat to place it where you want it.
6. Use **×** beside an item to remove it, then confirm. Reordering and removal save immediately; wait for **Saved**.
7. Choose **Print program** when the order is ready.

Review the print preview before sharing a copy. A program printed from an approved account includes private assignments and preparation notes visible to that account.

## Manage volunteers and songs

- **Ministry team:** Choose **Add volunteer**, enter the person's name, role, ministry, and optional contact information, then choose **Save changes**. Use **Edit** or **Delete** on an existing volunteer's card to update the directory.
- **Song library:** Choose **Add song**, enter the title and any musical key, hymn number, or notes, then choose **Save changes**. Use **Edit** or **Delete** to update an existing song.

Approved viewers can read these church records. Adding, editing, or deleting requires an approved editor or admin. Volunteer contact information and song notes remain private to approved church members.

## Publish content for visitors

An approved editor or admin chooses what appears publicly.

1. Edit a service and check **Publish this schedule, theme, and program for visitors**.
2. Review its title, date, time, theme, program titles, and durations; these will be visible to visitors.
3. Enter **Public attendance location (optional)** if visitors should see where to attend. Leave it blank to show **Location to be announced**. The separate **Church member location** remains private.
4. Choose **Save changes**.
5. To share an assignment, edit that individual program item, check **Show this assignment in the public program when its service is published**, and save. Service publication alone keeps assignments private.
6. To publish a song title in **Song library**, edit the song, check **Publish this song title for visitors**, and save. This is separate from the text you enter in a service program.

Check the result in a separate signed-out or private browser window. Choose **Refresh schedules** there to see your changes.

To stop publishing a service or song, uncheck its publication option and save. To hide only an assignment, uncheck that item's public-assignment option and save. Private preparation notes, volunteer contacts, song notes, account emails, member records, and backups are not included in public browsing.

## Find finished and past programs

Open **Service planner** and switch between:

- **Current plans:** Upcoming services and programs finished less than one calendar month ago, ordered by date and time.
- **Past programs:** Older services, with the most recent first. Open any program to revisit or print it; approved editors and admins can also edit it.

A **Program finished** banner appears the day after a service's date. Today's service has no finished banner, even if its start time has passed.

A service enters **Past programs** one calendar month after its date. For example, a September 19 service appears there on October 19. A January 31 service appears there on February 28, or February 29 in a leap year. Older services also leave the Overview calendar and current service count.

Plans are kept, so use **Past programs** instead of creating a replacement for a missing older plan. Rescheduling a past service to a future date returns it to **Current plans**. Published past programs remain visible to visitors until you turn off publication. Keep your device's date, time, and time zone correct.

## Manage members as an admin

1. Open **Admin workspace**. New requests appear under **Pending**.
2. Find the person using **Search members** or the **Status** filter. Choose **Refresh members** to check for new requests.
3. Select **Approve as**: **Viewer**, **Editor**, or **Admin**. Viewer is the default.
4. Choose **Approve** and confirm, or choose **Reject** and confirm.
5. Ask the person to choose **Check status** in their account to see the result.

For an approved member, select a **Church role**, choose **Save role**, and confirm. Choose **Revoke access** to remove their private workspace access. Use **Reconsider request** for a rejected or revoked account to return it to Pending, then review it again.

Another admin must manage your own role and access. The last approved admin cannot be removed or demoted; approve another trusted admin before changing that access.

## Export or restore a backup

### Export

Approved viewers, editors, and admins can choose **Export backup** to download `gather-backup.json`. It contains the full planner, including current and past services, assignments, preparation notes, volunteer contacts, and song details. Store it privately.

### Restore

1. Sign in as an approved editor or admin.
2. Export the current planner first, and coordinate the restore with other editors.
3. Choose **Import backup** and select a valid Gather JSON backup no larger than 10 MiB.
4. Confirm **Replace your current planner with this backup?** only when you intend to replace the shared planner, including its publication choices.
5. Wait for **Saved**, then review the restored entries and public content.

Import replaces the planner; it does not merge entries. A backup restores planner content, not user accounts or member approvals.

## Get help with common problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Editing buttons are missing | Check your approval status and role. An approved viewer needs an admin to grant Editor or Admin access. |
| Another person's changes are missing | Choose **Refresh data**, or **Refresh schedules** when browsing public content. |
| A save reports a conflict or **Not saved** | Refresh the planner, review the latest version, then apply your change again. |
| A save reports **Save not confirmed** | Check your connection, refresh, and inspect the plan before retrying; the change may already have reached the server. |
| A form reports an invalid date or duration | Correct the reported value and choose **Save changes** again. |
| A service is missing | Check **Past programs**, clear your search, and refresh. Visitors see only published services. |
| Verification or reset email is missing | Check spam and the address you entered. Contact your church admin if delivery still fails. |
| Sign-in reports an expired session or too many requests | Enable automatic date and time, close duplicate tabs, wait before retrying, and start a fresh sign-in from the normal Gather address. Contact your church admin if it continues. |
